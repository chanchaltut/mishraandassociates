# Managing your website — quick guide

Sign in at **https://www.mishranassociates.com/admin**, or use **Team login** at the bottom of any page.

## The two buttons that matter

1. **Save changes** (on each page). This stores your edits safely. Visitors don't see them yet.
2. **Publish** (top right). This puts everything you've saved on the website. The site updates in about **1–2 minutes**, and the panel tells you when it's live.

A gold dot next to a section in the menu means it has saved changes that are not published yet.

**Preview** shows the whole website with your saved changes before you publish. Only you see it.

## Good to know

- **Lists:** click an item to open it. Use the arrows to reorder it, the copy icon to duplicate it, and the bin to delete it. New blog and news items go at the top.
- **Text in [square brackets]** means "not filled in yet". It is hidden on the website. Replace it with the real text and it appears.
- **Images:** press *Upload*, or paste a link. Keep files under 10 MB.
- **Article text:** leave an empty line between paragraphs. A paragraph that starts with `**Bold text**` on its own line becomes a sub-heading.
- **Red fields** need fixing before you can save. The message under the field tells you what's wrong.
- If two people edit the same section at once, the second save is stopped (nothing gets overwritten). Reload and make your change again.
- If something looks wrong after publishing, go to **Publish history → Restore** the previous version.
